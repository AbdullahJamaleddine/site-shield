FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Within Ibadan we offer same-day delivery, provided every item on your order is in stock at the time you check out. For interstate orders, the timeline depends on your location — most deliveries land within 2–5 business days.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll confirm the shipping cost and timeline before you order.

What's the return policy?

Unworn items can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery, with tags still attached. Sale items are final.

Where are you located?

We're based in Ibadan, Oyo State. Pickup is available on request — just message us on WhatsApp to arrange a time.
